Hardgrain Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Test version. This draft covers the free beta. Items highlighted as “to be added before launch” will be filled in before the app is released on the App Store. In this beta, all data stays on your device: there is no sign-in, backup, sync, payment, or crash reporting yet.

Hardgrain ("the App," "we," "us") is published by [Developer name: to be added before launch]. This policy explains what information the App handles and how. Questions: vegavisionvante@gmail.com.

1. Information stored on your device

The App stores your profile details (fitness goal, fitness level, available equipment) and your workout logs on your device. We do not receive this information unless you turn on backup and sync.

2. Optional Sign in with Apple, backup, and sync

If you choose Sign in with Apple, we receive an account identifier from Apple and, if you allow it, your name and email (which may be an Apple private relay address). If you turn on backup or sync, your profile details and workout logs are stored with our hosting provider, [Backend provider: to be added before launch], so you can restore them or use them on another device. We use this information only to provide backup and sync.

3. Subscriptions

Pro subscriptions are bought and managed through Apple. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Apple tells us whether your subscription is active so we can unlock Pro features.

4. Crash reports

The App may collect basic crash and performance data (device model, operating system version, app version, and what happened when the App crashed) through [Crash reporting tool: to be added before launch, or this section removed]. This data is used only to find and fix bugs.

5. What we don't do

We do not show ads, use advertising trackers, sell your information, or share it for advertising. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

6. Apple Health

The current version of the App does not read from or write to Apple Health. If a future version adds Apple Health, we will ask your permission first. Health data will never be used for advertising or sold, and we'll update this policy before it ships.

7. Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help run the App (hosting and crash reporting), only as needed to provide those services, and when the law requires it.

8. Retention and deletion

Data on your device stays there until you delete it or remove the App. If you used backup or sync, you can delete your account and its backed-up data at any time in the App under [Settings > Delete Account: to be added before launch], or by emailing vegavisionvante@gmail.com. We delete backed-up data within [30: to be confirmed before launch] days of the request.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including California, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your information, and to know how it's used. Email vegavisionvante@gmail.com and we'll respond within the time the law requires.

10. Children

The App is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

11. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, but no system is completely secure.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. We'll post the new version here with a new "Last updated" date, and tell you in the App if the change is significant.
