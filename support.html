FAQ

How do I cancel Hardgrain Pro?

Go to Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions on your iPhone, choose Hardgrain, then tap Cancel Subscription. (Pro is coming soon. It is free during testing and nothing can be bought yet.)

How do I restore my purchase?

When Pro launches, use Restore purchases on the Pro screen in the app. There is nothing to restore during testing.

Do I need equipment?

No. There are no-equipment home plans, plus dumbbell and full-gym plans. Change your equipment any time in Settings.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, go to Settings › Delete my data. The beta has no accounts; everything is stored only on your device.

How do I install it on my iPhone?

Open the beta in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
