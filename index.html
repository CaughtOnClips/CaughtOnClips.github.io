◆ How you level

Train. Earn XP. Get stronger.

01 · DAILY QUEST

A quest every day

A short, no-equipment home set that scales with your level. Clear it before midnight to earn XP and attribute points.

02 · XP

Every rep counts

Log sets at home or in the gym. Reps, holds and runs all feed your XP bar, which goes toward your next level.

03 · STATS

Build your character

Spend points on STR, END, AGI, VIT and DIS. Your stats grow along with your real training.

04 · SKILLS

Unlock new plans

Reach stat goals to unlock skills, and each skill opens a new guided program, from bodyweight basics to loaded strength.
